  <FAQItem question="What is the right plan for me?">
    If you're an individual developer exploring AI-assisted coding, start with our **Free** plan. For professionals who need unlimited usage and advanced features, the **Pro** plan is ideal. Teams and organizations should consider **Business** for centralized billing, admin controls, and priority support.
  </FAQItem>

  <FAQItem question="What are my payment options?">
    We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and support both monthly and annual billing. Annual plans include a 20% discount. For Business plans, we also offer invoicing with NET 30 terms for qualifying organizations.
  </FAQItem>

  <FAQItem question="How does usage-based pricing work?">
    Usage-based pricing applies to AI requests beyond your plan's included allowance. You only pay for what you use, billed at the end of each billing cycle. You can set spending limits to control costs, and unused credits don't roll over. Real-time usage dashboards help you monitor consumption.
  </FAQItem>

  <FAQItem question="How can I see and manage Cursor's usage in my organization?">
    Organization admins can access the **Admin Dashboard** to view team-wide usage analytics, manage seats, set spending limits per user or team, and export detailed usage reports. You can also configure alerts for usage thresholds and automate seat provisioning via SSO.
  </FAQItem>

  <FAQItem question="How does Cursor use my data?">
    Your code and data are never used to train our models. We process code snippets only to generate suggestions, and this data is not stored beyond the immediate request. Business plans include additional privacy controls, SOC 2 compliance, and the option for zero data retention. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for details.
  </FAQItem>

  <FAQItem question="Where can I ask more questions?">
    Check our [documentation](/docs) for detailed guides, visit our [community forum](https://community.cursor.com) for discussions with other users, or [contact our support team](/contact) directly. Business customers have access to dedicated support channels with guaranteed response times.
  </FAQItem>
