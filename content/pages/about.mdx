# Our Story

We believe in making developers more productive through the power of AI. Our mission is to build tools that augment human creativity and accelerate software development.


<Hero variant="centered" />

## What We Do

We create cutting-edge AI-powered development tools that help engineers write better code faster. Our platform combines the best language models with deep code understanding to provide intelligent assistance at every step of the development process.

### Core Values

- **Developer First**: Every decision is made with developers in mind
- **Innovation**: Pushing the boundaries of what's possible with AI
- **Quality**: No compromises on code quality or user experience
- **Open**: Building in public and learning from the community

## Our Team

We're a team of engineers, researchers, and designers passionate about improving the developer experience. With backgrounds from leading tech companies and research institutions, we bring together diverse perspectives to solve hard problems.
