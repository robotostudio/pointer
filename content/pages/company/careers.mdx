## Application Process

1. **Apply**: Submit your application with your resume and a brief note about why you're interested
2. **Phone Screen**: 30-minute conversation with our recruiting team
3. **Technical Interview**: Dive deep into your expertise
4. **Team Interviews**: Meet the people you'll work with
5. **Offer**: Welcome to the team!

## How to Apply

Send your resume and a note about what excites you to **careers@example.com**

Include:
- Your resume/CV
- Links to your work (GitHub, portfolio, etc.)
- Why you want to join us
- What role you're interested in

**Don't see a role that fits?** We're always interested in meeting talented people. Send us your information and we'll keep you in mind for future opportunities.
