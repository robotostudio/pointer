# About Our Company

Founded in 2024, we've grown from a small team of engineers to a company serving millions of developers worldwide.

## Our Mission

To make every developer extraordinarily productive by combining human creativity with artificial intelligence.

## Our Values

### Innovation First

We push the boundaries of what's possible with AI and software development tools.

### Developer Obsessed

Every product decision starts with understanding developer needs and pain points.

### Built to Last

We create tools that developers can rely on for years to come, with a focus on stability and backward compatibility.

### Open & Transparent

We believe in building in public, sharing our learnings, and contributing back to the community.

## Timeline

**2024** - Founded with a vision to revolutionize coding with AI  
**2024 Q2** - Launched first beta to 100 developers  
**2024 Q4** - Reached 1 million users  
**2025** - Expanded to enterprise customers
